# Tokenized stocks

ROBANK lists two families. Neither is issued by ROBANK, and ROBANK cannot buy, sell, mint or redeem them.

## xStocks
- Issuer: Backed Assets (xStocks). Symbols end with `x` (e.g. `AAPLx`).
- Tokenized tracker certificates, not shares; no shareholder rights.
- ROBANK supports holdings on Ethereum, Arbitrum, Optimism and BNB Chain (ERC-20, 18 decimals) and Solana (Token-2022, 8 decimals).
- Eligibility, minting and redemption are set by the issuer and are unavailable in some countries.

## Robinhood Stock Tokens
- Issuer: Robinhood, on Robinhood Chain (4663). ERC-20 with contract-defined decimals.
- Each token tracks `multiplier` shares of the underlying (changes with corporate actions).

## Prices
Indicative reference = underlying last sale from the Nasdaq screener × multiplier. It can be delayed or missing; missing prices are shown as "no price" and excluded from totals. Never present it as a tradable quote.

## What users can do in ROBANK
See the catalog, see what they hold, receive tokens to the right address, and send tokens they hold. Nothing else.
